# Client gate — Legal / HR review pack

Self-contained HTML for Insulet Legal and People & Compliance to approve **simplified playbook language** and **contact routing** before live delivery.

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [`review-pack.html`](review-pack.html) | Open in a browser → Print / Save as PDF. Includes sign-off form, agenda glance, pre-read map, claims list, Handout 1 & 2. |

**Not included:** facilitator scripts, full slide decks, exercise mocks, pitch video.

**How to use**

1. Open `review-pack.html` locally (double-click or serve from this folder).
2. Send to reviewers or “Print → Save as PDF” and attach.
3. Capture completed sign-off (printed/PDF or email reply mirroring Section 1).
4. After approval, freeze contact edits into `handouts/` if anything changed.
