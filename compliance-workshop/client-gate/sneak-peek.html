Session 1 excerpt

Unlearning “At-Will” Assumptions

Foreign assumption Reality in Mexico
“We can part ways anytime.” No at-will. Cause or statutory severance.
“Contractors keep us flexible.” Core outsourcing restricted. Route to HR/Legal.
“Overtime is just scheduling.” Capped, pre-authorized, logged — mishandling creates claims.
Manager alert: Never say or write at-will, probationary at-will, or we can let you go anytime.

Session 1 excerpt

High-Risk Moment — Fixed-Term Expirations

  • Rule: Fixed-term is valid only while the real justification lasts.
  • Danger: Work continuing past the end date → auto-converts to indefinite by law.
  • Action: Flag HR weeks before expiry.
Escalate now: Never tell someone to “keep working while paperwork processes.”

Session 1 excerpt

Overtime — Compliance, Not a Side Deal

  • Extraordinary, pre-authorized, voluntary, logged in Workday (same day or week).
  • Cap: 12 hours/week (4 hrs/day, max 4 days).
  • Absolute ban: No flat cash, gift cards, allowances, or informal comp time instead of overtime pay.
  • Managers authorize and record hours — Payroll applies premiums.

Session 2 excerpt

When Managers Must Stop

  • Exit: Never communicate a termination or discuss severance alone → ER / Legal / CFCRL.
  • Harassment / bullying: Recognize → Report immediately → Confidentiality → Zero retaliation. Never “keep it off the record.”
  • Documentation: Facts and dates — not labels. Poor performance alone rarely equals legal cause.
Live sessions test when to stop and escalate — not how to calculate pay or draft contracts.
