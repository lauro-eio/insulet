## Slide 3: Performance Management & Progressive Discipline

**Title:** Performance Management & Factual Logging

**Visual / Layout:** Step-by-step progressive discipline chevron diagram paired with a "Fact vs. Opinion" warning box.

**Slide Content:**

* **The Reality:** Poor performance alone rarely constitutes legal cause for separation under Mexican labor law.


* **Progressive Discipline Workflow:**
1. Set clear, written expectations and standards.


2. Document gaps with specific, dated facts in real time.


3. Partner with Employee Relations (ER) for verbal/written warnings or formal PIPs.




* **Factual Logging Rules:**
* Record dates, specific events, and operational impacts.


* Avoid labels, subjective opinions, or personal assumptions.





---

## Slide 4: High-Risk Clock & Consistency

**Title:** The 30-Day Misconduct Clock & Consistency

**Visual / Layout:** Hourglass visual highlighting the 30-day window, paired with a balance scale icon for team consistency.

**Slide Content:**

* **The 30-Day Statutory Misconduct Clock:**
* If an employee commits a specific act of misconduct, Mexican law requires prompt employer action.


* Formal notice must generally be issued within **30 days** of becoming aware of the act.


* **The Risk:** Sitting on misconduct forfeits the right to rely on it as legal cause.




* **Consistency Prevents Discrimination Claims:**
* Treat similar conduct and performance issues identically across your team.


* Inconsistent enforcement creates pretext claims, turning a performance matter into a high-risk discrimination claim.

## Slide 6: Justified vs. Unjustified Separations

**Title:** Termination Types & Aggregate Severance Exposure

**Visual / Layout:** Comparison matrix of separation types with a warning banner highlighting aggregate salary exposure.

**Slide Content:**

| Separation Type | What It Means | Financial Exposure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Justified (*Con Causa*)** | Separation for a specific cause recognized by the FLL, supported by documented proof.

 | No statutory severance if proof holds up (extremely high legal bar).

 |
| **Unjustified (*Sin Causa*)** | Any exit without proven FLL cause, including "poor fit" or undocumented performance.

 | **Full Statutory Severance** (3 months salary + seniority pay + accrued benefits).

 |
| **Mutual Agreement** | Negotiated exit agreed by both parties and ratified before conciliation authorities.

 | Negotiated package; provides legal certainty.

 |

> **Manager Alert:** Severance is calculated on **aggregate salary** (base pay + bonuses, allowances, and benefits). Actual exposure is far higher than base-salary estimates.

## Slide 7: Mandatory Pre-Trial Conciliation (CFCRL)

**Title:** The Exit Workflow & Pre-Trial Conciliation

**Visual / Layout:** Workflow diagram showing the path from internal ER review to CFCRL ratification, ending with an explicit "STOP" icon.

**Slide Content:**

* **Labor Justice Framework (CFCRL):**
* Mexican disputes pass through mandatory pre-trial conciliation at the Federal Center for Conciliation and Labor Registration.


* Ratifying negotiated exits at the conciliation center makes agreements binding and legally closed.




* **Manager Termination Decision Rules:**
* **Rule 1:** STOP before having any exit conversation with an employee.


* **Rule 2:** Never say "you're fired," suggest a resignation, or discuss severance terms alone.


* **Rule 3:** Every separation must be structured and led by Employee Relations and Legal.

## Slide 9: Harassment & Complaint Handling: The Inaction Trap

**Title:** Harassment Protocols: Recognize, Report, Retaliate Never

**Visual / Layout:** Process flow diagram illustrating the manager's 4 duties upon receiving a complaint, paired with an "Inaction Trap" alert box.

**Slide Content:**

* **Your 4 Mandatory Duties:**
1. **Recognize:** Take every report of harassment, violence, or discrimination seriously.


2. **Report Immediately:** Forward the complaint to Employee Relations within the first hour.


3. **Protect Confidentiality:** Share information strictly on a need-to-know basis.


4. **Never Retaliate:** Zero tolerance for adverse actions against anyone raising a concern.




* **The Inaction Trap:**
* If a manager ignores or mishandles a complaint, the employee can terminate employment with cause attributable to the employer.


* **Result:** The company owes full statutory severance equivalent to an unjustified firing, plus regulatory fines.

## Slide 13: Scenario 3 Debrief & Answer Key

**Title:** Scenario 3 Debrief: Discipline & Exit Risk

**Visual / Layout:** Green checkmark on correct answer, red hazard icons on traps.

**Slide Content:**

* **Correct Answer:** **C — Halt termination discussion and contact Employee Relations**.


* **Why Option A Is Dangerous:** Undocumented poor performance is not recognized cause under Mexican law; proceeding results in an unjustified dismissal claim.


* **Why Option B Is Dangerous:** Informal cash settlements bypass payroll, underestimate aggregate severance exposure, and fail to secure binding legal releases.


* **Escalation Path:** Partner with ER immediately to build factual documentation or structure a compliant exit.
