## Session 1: Hiring, Contracts & Work Hours (90 mins)

### 1. Session Setup & Opening (10 mins)

* Establish session norms, interactive polling tools, and the golden rule: *When in doubt, never act alone*.

### 2. The Mexican Framework & Contract Risks (25 mins)

* **Ch 1: The "No At-Will" Reality (10 mins)**
  * **Rule:** FLL is strictly worker-protective; the employer carries the full burden of proof.
  * **Red Flag:** Using terms like "at-will," "probationary at-will," or "we can part ways anytime" in verbal or written communication.
  * **Escalation:** Route all new-hire or trial-period concerns to your HRBP before acting.

* **Ch 2: Employment Contracts & Subcontracting Restrictions (15 mins)**
  * **Rule:** Indefinite contracts are the mandatory default; fixed-term roles require strict legal justification. Subcontracting core operations is prohibited.
  * **Red Flag:** Letting a fixed-term contract lapse while work continues (triggers auto-conversion to permanent status) or engaging "contractors" for recurring company work.
  * **Escalation:** Flag fixed-term expirations to HR weeks in advance; route all contractor inquiries through HR/Legal.

### 3. Working Hours & Overtime Compliance (25 mins)

* **Ch 3: Hours, Overtime Caps & Payment Prohibitions (20 mins)**
  * **Rule:** Shifts have statutory hour limits; overtime is capped (max 12 hrs/week, 4 hrs/day) and requires pre-authorization and statutory premiums.
  * **Red Flag:** Off-the-clock work, informal comp-time agreements, or offering flat cash/bonuses instead of proper overtime pay.
  * **Escalation:** Report actual hours in Workday daily/weekly; contact HR/Payroll before implementing unusual schedules or exceeding weekly caps.

* **Ch 4: Payroll & Benefits Take-Home (5 mins)**
  * **Rule:** Manager responsibility is accurate time and leave approval; HR/Payroll owns calculations and disbursements.
  * **Red Flag:** Any request for off-payroll, cash-in-hand, or informal financial adjustments.
  * **Escalation:** Route all employee pay inquiries directly to Payroll.

### 4. Scenario-Based Assessment Block (20 mins)

* **Scenario 1 (Contracts):** A fixed-term project employee's end date is approaching, but project work remains. What action must the manager take?
* **Scenario 2 (Overtime):** An employee agrees to work extra hours over the weekend in exchange for taking next Friday off instead of overtime pay. How should the manager handle this?

### 5. Session 1 Wrap-Up & Handout Distribution (10 mins)

* Distribute **Handout 1:** *Statutory Benefits Summary & Payroll Contact Guide*.

## Session 2: Discipline, Exits & Workplace Safety (90 mins)

### 1. Session Setup & Session 1 Recap (10 mins)

* Re-anchor the escalation protocol and review core themes from Session 1.

### 2. Performance, Discipline & Separations (25 mins)

* **Ch 5: Progressive Discipline & Documenting Misconduct (10 mins)**
  * **Rule:** Disciplinary action requires objective, written documentation created in real-time. Misconduct carries a strict 30-day window for statutory cause.
  * **Red Flag:** Relying on vague claims ("poor fit"), sitting on misconduct for weeks, or applying inconsistent standards across team members.
  * **Escalation:** Partner with Employee Relations immediately upon identifying performance or conduct issues.

* **Ch 6: Justified vs. Unjustified Separation (15 mins)**
  * **Rule:** Ending employment without proven FLL cause triggers full statutory severance (calculated on aggregate salary) and mandatory CFCRL pre-trial conciliation.
  * **Red Flag:** Communicating a termination, telling an employee "it's not working out," or discussing severance terms without HR/Legal involvement.
  * **Escalation:** Halt all exit discussions; involve ER and your HRBP prior to taking any separation steps.

### 3. Safety, Anti-Discrimination & Compliance Matrix (25 mins)

* **Ch 7: Harassment Protocols & Psychosocial Risk (15 mins)**
  * **Rule:** Employers must enforce zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, and workplace violence under NOM-035/037 and federal protocols.
  * **Red Flag:** Conducting independent informal investigations, ignoring employee safety complaints, or taking adverse actions against reporters (retaliation).
  * **Escalation:** Report any discrimination or harassment complaint immediately to Employee Relations.

* **Ch 8 & 9: Non-Compliance Risks & Emergency Escalations (10 mins)**
  * **Rule:** Non-compliance leads to severe financial exposure, statutory penalties, and legal liability.
  * **Red Flag:** Deviating from established company compliance procedures.
  * **Escalation:** Refer directly to the Quick Reference Escalation Directory.

### 4. Scenario-Based Assessment Block (20 mins)

* **Scenario 3 (Discipline/Exit):** A manager wants to separate from an employee for ongoing poor performance, but no written coaching or warnings exist. What is the correct next step?
* **Scenario 4 (Harassment):** An employee informally reports workplace bullying but asks the manager to "keep it quiet and not report it." How must the manager respond?

### 5. Final Wrap-Up & Resource Distribution (10 mins)

* Distribute **Handout 2:** *Consequence Risk Matrix & Manager Escalation Directory*.
