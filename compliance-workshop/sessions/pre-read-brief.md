# Pre-Read Brief — Mexico Manager Compliance Workshop

**Status:** LOCKED (participant prep)  
**Guide:** [`../source/Mexico-Manager-Compliance-Guide_FV.clean.md`](../source/Mexico-Manager-Compliance-Guide_FV.clean.md) (or client PDF)  
**Workshop:** [`agenda.md`](agenda.md) — 2 × 90 minutes

Live sessions teach **Rule → Red Flag → Escalation**, not a full chapter walkthrough. Pre-reading keeps the ~50 teach minutes on recognition and stop-points. Come ready to discuss, not to hear the guide read aloud.

**How to use this brief:** Complete the **Required** chapters before each session. **Skim / optional** items support depth if you have time. Bring questions to class or route them to your HRBP / Payroll / ER as listed in the handouts.

## Session 1 — Hiring, Contracts & Work Hours

**Pre-read deadline:** Before Session 1 start  
**In session:** Framework, contracts, overtime bans, Handout 1  
**Handout (distributed in class):** [`../handouts/handout-01-statutory-benefits.md`](../handouts/handout-01-statutory-benefits.md)

| Priority | Guide section | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Required** | How to Use This Guide (golden rule + Manager Alert / Escalate Now markers) | Sets the escalation mindset used all workshop |
| **Required** | **Ch 1** — Why Mexico Is Different | No at-will; employer burden of proof; assumption resets |
| **Required** | **Ch 2** — Employment Contracts | Indefinite default; fixed-term auto-conversion; outsourcing bans |
| **Required** | **Ch 3** — Working Hours & Overtime | Caps, pre-authorization, no bonus/comp-time substitutions |
| **Skim** | **Ch 4** — Payroll & Statutory Benefits | Ownership split only; calculations stay with Payroll (Handout 1) |

**Focus while reading**

* Banned language: “at-will,” “we can part ways anytime”
* What happens if a fixed-term employee keeps working past the end date
* Why informal overtime deals (cash, gift cards, Friday off) are prohibited
* What you own vs. what HR/Payroll owns (time/leave vs. formulas)

**Do not try to memorize:** Overtime premium bands, Aguinaldo/PTU formulas, cutoff calendars — confirm with Payroll; Handout 1 covers routing.

## Session 2 — Discipline, Exits & Workplace Safety

**Pre-read deadline:** Before Session 2 start  
**In session:** Documentation, 30-day misconduct clock, separations, harassment escalation, Handout 2  
**Handout (distributed in class):** [`../handouts/handout-02-consequence-matrix.md`](../handouts/handout-02-consequence-matrix.md)

| Priority | Guide section | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Required** | **Ch 5** — Performance Management & Discipline | Factual logs; progressive discipline; 30-day misconduct window; consistency |
| **Required** | **Ch 6** — Termination | Justified vs. unjustified; aggregate severance shape; CFCRL; never exit alone |
| **Required** | **Ch 7** — Anti-Discrimination, Harassment & Workplace Safety | Recognize → Report → Confidentiality → Zero retaliation; inaction cost |
| **Skim** | **Ch 8** — Consequence Matrix | Stakes overview; Handout 2 is the take-home matrix |
| **Skim** | **Ch 9** — Quick Reference (contacts + red flags) | Directory reinforced on Handout 2; confirm live contacts with People |

**Focus while reading**

* Facts/dates vs. labels (“poor attitude”)
* Why undocumented poor performance is not automatic legal cause
* The 30-day misconduct clock (Session 2 only — not fixed-term expiry)
* Stop before any exit / severance conversation
* Never investigate harassment “off the record”

**Do not try to memorize:** Exact severance components or NOM questionnaire mechanics — ER/Legal and Handout 2 carry exposure shape and routing.
