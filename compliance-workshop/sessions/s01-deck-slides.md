## Slide 4: Unlearning "At-Will" Assumptions

**Title:** Unlearning "At-Will" Assumptions  
**Visual / Layout:** Side-by-side comparison table. Red X for assumptions, green check for Mexican reality.

**Slide Content:**

| Foreign / At-Will Assumption | The Reality in Mexico (Ley Federal del Trabajo) |
| --- | --- |
| "We can part ways anytime if it's not working." | **No At-Will Employment.** Every separation requires proven cause or statutory severance. |
| "I'll hire a contractor to keep things flexible." | **Strict Outsourcing Restrictions.** Subcontracting core operations is generally prohibited. |
| "Trial periods let us test people without risk." | **Day-One Protections.** Employees accrue full statutory rights and benefits from Day 1. |
| "Overtime is just a scheduling detail." | **Strictly Capped & Premium Pay.** Mishandling overtime creates severe claim liability. |

> **Manager Alert:** Never say or write *"at-will"*, *"probationary at-will"*, or *"we can let you go anytime"* to or about a Mexico-based employee.

## Slide 6: Choosing the Right Contract

**Title:** Choosing the Right Contract  
**Visual / Layout:** Process flow diagram — contract selection tree leading to HR execution.

**Slide Content:**

* **Indefinite (Full-Time) Contract:** **The Default.** Applies to standard ongoing roles. Must be signed on Day 1.
* **Fixed-Term / Specified-Task Contract:** **The Exception.** Allowed *only* for genuine, temporary, and verifiable reasons (e.g., maternity leave cover, defined project end). Requires Legal approval.
* **Trial / Initial Training Periods:** Evaluation windows built into indefinite contracts. Full pay, social security (IMSS), and benefits apply immediately.
* **Contractors / Subcontracting (*Subcontratación*):** **Prohibited** for core business activities since the 2021 labor reform. Never engage contractors directly — STOP and route to HR/Legal.

## Slide 7: High-Risk Moment: Fixed-Term Expirations

**Title:** High-Risk Moment: Fixed-Term Expirations  
**Visual / Layout:** Large warning graphic with escalation timeline.

**Slide Content:**

* **The Rule:** A fixed-term contract is valid *only* while its specific underlying justification exists.
* **The Danger:** If the contract end date passes and the employee continues working for even one day, the relationship **automatically converts by law** into an indefinite (permanent) full-time contract.
* **Manager Action Required:**
  * Actively track project timelines.
  * Flag headcount needs to your HRBP **weeks before** a fixed-term contract expires.
  * If the role should end, work must stop immediately on the exact expiration date.

> **Escalate Now:** Never informally tell an employee to "keep working while paperwork processes".

## Slide 8: Working Hours & Overtime Compliance

**Title:** Working Hours & Overtime Compliance  
**Visual / Layout:** Two-column split (Left: Shift Limits; Right: Overtime Caps & Bans).

**Slide Content:**

* **Shift Limits (Statutory Max):**
  * **Day Shift:** 8 hrs/day (40 hrs/wk max)
  * **Night Shift:** 7 hrs/day (40 hrs/wk max)
  * **Mixed Shift:** 7.5 hrs/day

* **Overtime Caps & Rules:**
  * Overtime must be extraordinary, pre-authorized, voluntary, and logged in Workday **the same day or week**.
  * Maximum **12 hours/week** (capped at 4 hrs/day, max 4 days/week).
  * **Absolute Ban:** Never substitute overtime with flat cash, gift cards, allowances, or informal comp time.
  * Lack of precise time records is interpreted unfavorably against the employer.

> **Footnote:** Payroll automatically applies required statutory double/triple payment premiums during pay runs. Refer to Handout 1 for benefit and premium details — do not calculate premiums live.

## Slide 13: Scenario 1 Debrief: Contract Risk

**Title:** Scenario 1 Debrief: Contract Risk  
**Visual / Layout:** Green checkmark on correct answer; red hazard icons on traps.

**Slide Content:**

* **Correct Answer:** **B — Immediately halt work on Friday until HR and Legal issue a formal extension or contract change.**
* **Why Option A Is Dangerous:** Allowing work past the expiration date triggers **automatic conversion** to an indefinite (permanent) employment contract by law.
* **Why Option C Is Illegal:** Reclassifying core employees as contractors violates strict 2021 subcontracting bans.
* **Escalation Path:** Contact your HRBP weeks before a fixed-term contract expires to assess headcount needs and document justifications.

## Slide 15: Scenario 2 Debrief: Overtime Compliance

**Title:** Scenario 2 Debrief: Overtime Compliance  
**Visual / Layout:** Green checkmark on correct answer; red hazard icons on traps.

**Slide Content:**

* **Correct Answer:** **C — Log all actual hours and notify HR/Payroll.**
* **Why Option A & B Are Dangerous:** Employees cannot waive statutory rights. Flat bonuses or gift cards are treated by courts as wage underpayment, leaving the company liable for back pay, premiums, and fines.
* **The Escalation Trigger:** Exceeding 12 weekly overtime hours breaches legal limits and requires immediate validation by Legal and Payroll.
