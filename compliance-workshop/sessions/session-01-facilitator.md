Mexico is one of the most employee-protective jurisdictions in the world, and the burden of proof rests entirely on us as the employer. Today isn't about turning you into a labor lawyer—it's about teaching you the exact moments to stop and escalate to HR and Legal before taking action."

## 2. Module 1: The Mexican Framework & Contract Risks (25 mins)

* **What to Present (Slides):**
  * Side-by-side contrast: Foreign Assumptions vs. Mexican Legal Reality (No "at-will" employment; rights accrue from Day 1, even in trial periods).
  * Visual Decision Flow: Default Indefinite Contracts vs. Fixed-Term exceptions & Outsourcing bans.
  * Red Warning Callout: The Fixed-Term Auto-Conversion Rule.

* **What to Say (Script):**
  > "Never say or write 'at-will' or 'we can let you go anytime'. These phrases are legally unenforceable against the employee and can be used as evidence of an unlawful dismissal intent in labor courts.
  >
  > Indefinite full-time contracts are our legal default. Fixed-term contracts require actual, verifiable facts—like covering a maternity leave—not just a desire to 'keep options open'. If a fixed-term contract ends and the employee continues working for even one day, that relationship automatically converts by law into a permanent full-time role.
  >
  > Lastly, subcontracting or hiring 'contractors' for core company operations is generally prohibited by law since the 2021 labor reform. If you need a contractor, STOP immediately and route it to HR and Legal."

* **What to Do (Facilitation):**
  * Run a 2-minute "Banned Words" spot-check: Display sample manager emails and have participants call out risky phrasing (e.g., "probationary period at-will").
  * *Instructor Note:* Emphasize flagging expiring fixed-term contracts to HR **weeks in advance**—do not quote a statutory 30-day clock here (that clock applies only to misconduct notice in Session 2).

## 3. Module 2: Working Hours & Overtime Compliance (25 mins)

* **What to Present (Slides):**
  * Shift Limits Table: Day (8h/40h wk), Night (7h/40h wk), Mixed (7.5h).
  * Statutory Overtime Caps: Maximum 12 extraordinary hours/week, capped at 4 hours/day.
  * Red Flag Box: Absolute Prohibition on Overtime Substitutions.
  * Handout 1 Preview: Standard Statutory Benefits & Ownership Matrix.

* **What to Say (Script):**
  > "Overtime in Mexico is a legal compliance matter, not just a scheduling detail. Overtime must be voluntary, authorized by you in advance, and logged accurately in Workday.
  >
  > Here is the critical manager trap: You cannot offer comp time, flat cash allowances, or discretionary 'bonuses' to cover extra hours. Replacing overtime pay with off-record arrangements exposes the company to back-pay penalties and claims.
  >
  > Regarding routine benefits like Aguinaldo or PTU—review Handout 1 after this session. Your role as a manager is simple: approve hours and leave accurately; HR and Payroll handle all calculations and disbursements."

* **What to Do (Facilitation):**
  * Display a mock text exchange where a manager offers an employee Friday off in exchange for working extra weekend hours. Ask the group to spot the violation (informal comp-time trade).
  * Reinforce internal time-tracking expectations: Log and review hours in Workday within the same day or week.
  * *Instructor Note:* If questions drift into premium bands or Aguinaldo/PTU math, redirect to Handout 1 / Payroll — do not spend live time on calculation mechanics.

## 4. Interactive Scenario Assessments (20 mins)

* **What to Say (before polls):**
  > "We aren't testing if you can calculate overtime pay or draft a contract. We are testing if you know the exact moment to stop and escalate."

* **What to Do (Facilitation):** Launch poll → Display results → Call out the trap → Reinforce the escalation path.

### Scenario 1: The Expiring Fixed-Term Contract

* **Prompt:** A project employee's 6-month fixed-term contract expires next Friday, but project deliverables are delayed and work remains. The manager tells the employee informally to "keep working while we sort out the paperwork." What is the legal risk and correct action?

* **Live Poll Options:**
  * A) Allow them to work; paper hours can be backdated once approved.
  * B) Immediately halt work on Friday until HR and Legal issue a formal extension or contract change.
  * C) Reclassify them as an independent contractor to finish the project.

* **Instructor Debrief & Key:**
  * **Correct Answer:** **B**.
  * **The Trap:** Option A causes the contract to auto-convert into an indefinite (permanent) employment relationship by law. Option C violates strict 2021 outsourcing bans.
  * **Escalation Path:** Flag headcount needs to HR weeks prior to expiration.

### Scenario 2: The Busy Week & Off-Record Deal

* **Prompt:** During a high-volume week, a Mexico-based employee completes 14 hours of extra work. To help the department budget, the employee offers to accept a flat $100 USD "gift card" instead of submitting overtime hours in Workday.

* **Live Poll Options:**
  * A) Accept the offer since the employee proposed it voluntarily.
  * B) Log 9 hours as overtime in Workday and pay the remaining 5 hours via a flat bonus.
  * C) Log all actual hours worked in Workday, apply legal double/triple pay premiums, and consult Legal/Payroll regarding the weekly cap breach.

* **Instructor Debrief & Key:**
  * **Correct Answer:** **C**.
  * **The Trap:** Options A and B create severe liability. Voluntariness does not waive statutory rights. "Bonus" substitutions are treated by labor courts as underpayment, leaving the company liable for back pay plus penalties. Exceeding 12 weekly overtime hours requires immediate Legal/Payroll validation.
