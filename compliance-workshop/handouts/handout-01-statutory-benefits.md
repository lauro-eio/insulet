## 1. Statutory Benefits Overview

In Mexico, statutory benefits are mandated by the Federal Labor Law (*Ley Federal del Trabajo* or FLL). They are non-waivable rights that accrue from Day 1 of employment, including during trial or training periods.

| Statutory Benefit | Statutory Minimum Requirement | Manager Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aguinaldo** (Christmas Bonus) | Mandatory year-end bonus of **30 days’ salary**, paid by December each year (Payroll calculates per contract/entity). | Plan workloads, approvals, and schedules early so December payroll is not disrupted. |
| **Vacation & Vacation Premium** | Vacation: generally granted after 12 months of service; employees typically have up to 18 months to use accrued vacation. **Vacation premium (*Prima Vacacional*):** mandatory 25% premium on vacation pay. | Approve leave fairly and in Workday; encourage timely rest; do not pressure employees to forgo accrued vacation. |
| **PTU** (Profit Sharing) | Statutory employee profit sharing within the annual statutory window. | No direct action. Route eligibility and payout questions to HR/Payroll. |
| **IMSS** (Social Security) | Mandatory enrollment for medical, risk, disability, and retirement coverage. | Notify HR of confirmed start dates in time for Day 1 registration. |
| **INFONAVIT** (Housing Fund) | Mandatory employer contributions; may include payroll deductions for employee housing credits. | No direct action. Managed by HR and Payroll. |
| **Sunday Premium** (*Prima Dominical*) | Additional **25% premium** on daily salary when the regular schedule includes Sunday work. | Factor Sunday work into scheduling; confirm patterns with Payroll. |

## 2. Manager vs. HR / Payroll Ownership Matrix

Managers trigger decisions that impact payroll accuracy, but managers do not calculate payroll. Use this matrix to understand ownership boundaries:

| Task / Activity | Manager Owns | HR / Payroll / Finance Owns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time & Overtime Tracking** | Pre-authorizing extraordinary hours; logging and reviewing actual hours in Workday within the same day or week. | Calculating statutory overtime premiums, verifying weekly caps, and processing pay runs. |
| **Leave & Vacations** | Approving leave promptly in Workday; planning schedule coverage. | Calculating accruals, vacation premium disbursements, and legal usage windows. |
| **New-Hire Onboarding** | Timely submission of approved requisitions, offer details, and confirmed start dates. | IMSS/INFONAVIT registration, enrollment, and Day 1 contract execution. |
| **Employee Pay Questions** | Listening, acknowledging, and routing immediately to HR/Payroll. | Reviewing pay stubs, withholdings, and providing resolution. |

## 3. Critical Overtime & Payroll Compliance Rules

> **MANAGER ALERT: INFORMAL PAYROLL ARRANGEMENTS ARE ILLEGAL**  
> Never suggest, approve, or tolerate off-payroll arrangements, cash-in-hand payments, gift cards, or “we’ll true it up later” promises. Every peso of compensation must pass through proper payroll with statutory tax and social security contributions.

### Key overtime rules

1. **No substitutions:** Do not replace overtime pay with discretionary bonuses, flat allowances, informal comp time, or time off in lieu. Courts treat improper substitutions as underpayment.
2. **Mandatory logging:** Log all hours accurately in Workday. In disputes, missing or incomplete records weigh against the employer.
3. **Weekly caps:** Maximum **12 extraordinary hours per week** (4 hours/day, max 4 days/week). Exceeding the cap requires Legal and Payroll review before implementation.
4. **Premiums:** Payroll applies required statutory overtime premiums during the pay run. Managers do not calculate double/triple rates—verify recorded hours and approvals before payroll cutoff.
