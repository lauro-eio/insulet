## 1. Consequence Matrix for Non-Compliance

Use this matrix to understand **why** compliance procedures and immediate escalation matter — not to quote a dollar amount. Exposure bands are directional only.

| Manager Action / Misstep | Legal & Regulatory Risk | Financial & Structural Exposure | Reputational Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Misclassifying an employee as a contractor / improper outsourcing** | Authority reclassification; sanctions under 2021 subcontracting reforms | **High** — back pay, unpaid benefits, retroactive IMSS/INFONAVIT contributions, fines | Medium |
| **Unrecorded or unpaid overtime / “bonus” substitutions** | STPS inspection findings; employee labor claims | **Medium–High** — back pay at statutory premiums + interest and fines | Medium |
| **Letting a fixed-term contract lapse while work continues** | Automatic conversion to indefinite (permanent) employment | **Medium** — loss of fixed-term protections; future severance liability | Low |
| **Terminating without documented legal cause** | Unjustified dismissal claim | **High** — full statutory **aggregate** severance (base + bonuses, allowances, and benefits in kind; shape of exposure — Payroll/Legal calculate) + costs | High |
| **Ignoring or mishandling a harassment / discrimination report** | Employee rescission with cause attributable to the employer; authority action | **High** — full statutory severance equivalent + regulatory fines | Very High |
| **Off-payroll, cash, or informal compensation** | Tax, IMSS, and INFONAVIT violations | **High** — assessments, retroactive contributions, fines, personal exposure | High |
| **Omitting NOM-035 / mandatory workplace protocols** | STPS inspection findings | **Low–Medium** — administrative fines indexed to UMA | Medium |
| **Inconsistent discipline across the team** | Discrimination / pretext claims | **Medium–High** — severance + potential damages | High |

**Note:** Fines and indemnities in Mexico are frequently indexed to the UMA (*Unidad de Medida y Actualización*) or minimum-wage multiples, both of which rise annually. Treat prior figures as stale.

## 2. Red Flags: Stop and Escalate Immediately

If you encounter any of the following, **stop all independent action** and escalate immediately to Employee Relations, your HRBP, or Legal:

* **Contract expiration:** A fixed-term end date is within weeks, or has passed while the employee continues working.
* **Overtime thresholds:** Overtime without prior authorization, or approaching the statutory 12-hour weekly cap.
* **Informal deals:** Anyone proposes cash, gift cards, allowances, bonuses, or informal comp time instead of logging overtime in Workday.
* **Contemplating exit:** You are considering ending employment for any reason (performance, restructuring, or conduct). Exits route through ER/Legal and CFCRL conciliation — never DIY.
* **Safety & workplace reports:** Any report of harassment, discrimination, workplace violence, bullying, or retaliation.
* **Differential treatment:** You are asked or tempted to discipline one employee differently from a comparable colleague for similar conduct.
